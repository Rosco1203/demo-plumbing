Quick answers

What are your hours?

The office is open Monday–Friday, 8 AM–5 PM Eastern. On-call plumbers handle emergencies 24/7.

Is there a service-call fee?

The office confirms the service-call fee when you request service. The plumber confirms all pricing before any work.

Do you handle slab leaks?

Yes. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water, or a sudden jump in your water bill. Call the office to request leak detection.

Do you pump septic tanks?

We don’t pump septic tanks, but the office can refer you to a septic company.

What should I do while I wait?

Keep people and pets away from water or sewage and anything electrical. If you know where your main water shut-off is and can reach it safely, you can turn it off. If you smell gas, leave the home and call 911.
